VERIFY THE RECEIPT

Read the document at its own level of authority.

Identify the speaker

A company guide describes its own practice. A staff alert, advisory, policy recommendation and individual statement each have a different purpose.

Check the linked question

A publication can support one claim and add context to another. Follow the question link below each claim to see that relationship.

Watch the date

Publication dates mark when a document appeared. They are not timestamps for a platform’s present reserves, an account’s protection or local law today.
