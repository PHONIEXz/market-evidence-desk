# Brag plan: Market Evidence Desk

## Product and angle

Market Evidence Desk is a research interface for published, dated evidence in Sanity. A reader can open a question, inspect linked claims and their source scope, compare positions, then follow a guided dossier in the Evidence Atlas. The video follows that exact path. Its hook is the project's own line: **Make the claim. Show the receipt.** No live price, investment advice, automated trading, or currently enabled hosted agent is implied.

## Source and visual identity

- Source: `web/index.html`, `web/research.html`, `web/compare.html`, `web/atlas.html`, `web/style.css` on `feat/live-sanity-research-desk` at `f9e56b7`.
- UI material: the project's actual headlines, question about a reserve snapshot, claim stance labels, original-publication link, and dossier headings. The depicted UI is a compact editorial reconstruction, not a screen recording or a claim that the example output was fetched live.
- Palette: background `#0a1019`, panel `#121c28`, text `#f2f5f6`, mint `#83e5c5`, gold `#e9bd70`; Inter/system sans. Reproduce the project's triangular source-trail mark.
- Tone: polished, quiet product film. Landscape 1280 × 720, 21 seconds. Voiceover off.
- User flow: choose a research question → inspect a dated claim and its source → compare scope → open the atlas to see remaining limits.

## Audio and distribution

Original 21-second instrumental bed generated by `composition/assets/music/generate_bed.py`, with two synthesized interaction accents embedded at scene changes. This avoids unverified rights for bundled third-party music. Approximate beat grid: 110 BPM; visual scene cuts are driven by reading time, not exact beat sync. Keep the music restrained under all copy. No external audio assets or narration.

Share copy: “Built Market Evidence Desk to make research easier to question: dated claims, original publications, and the limits left visible. Make the claim. Show the receipt.”
